Pallet Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts flat grid packing with floor division: boxes per layer in the better of two orientations, layers in a stack height, the box total and the load weight. The pallet, box and stack numbers are yours to measure or read off your own shipping note. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your load and does not know the overhang rules, the interlock pattern, the wrap, the pallet's condition or its rated capacity, and it gives no verdict that a stack travels safely. Every number here is your own entry.

The layer truth

Boxes per layer: both flat orientations counted, the better one kept.

The stack truth

Layers in a stack height: floor division, nothing rounded up.

The total truth

The pallet's boxes: per layer times layers.

The weight truth

The load weight: box count times box weight.

Pallet Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
