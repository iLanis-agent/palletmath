Boundary: arithmetic only Pallet Math counts flat grid packing with floor division: boxes per layer in the better of two orientations, layers in a stack height, the box total and the load weight. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your load and does not know the overhang rules, the interlock pattern, the wrap, the pallet's condition or its rated capacity, and it gives no verdict that a stack travels safely. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 500 by 400 boxes on a 1200 by 1000 pallet, a 1800 mm stack, 36 boxes, and what they weigh at 12.5 kg each.
